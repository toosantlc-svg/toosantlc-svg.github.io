ABOUT TLC

A place to learn, communicate and grow.

At Toosan Language Center, we believe learning a language should be practical, engaging and encouraging. Our goal is to create an environment where learners can develop real communication skills while building confidence step by step.

Whether you are learning English for school, work, travel, exams or personal development, TLC is here to help you move forward.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hossein Ahmadi

Founder & Director, Toosan Language Center

With more than 20 years of experience in English language education, Hossein Ahmadi has worked in a wide range of areas in language teaching and professional development.

His professional experience includes working as an official translator and as an instructor specializing in IELTS, TOEFL, PTE and MSRT preparation. He has also worked as a teacher trainer, supporting English teachers in developing their teaching knowledge, classroom skills and professional practice.

As the founder of Toosan Language Center, his aim is to create a supportive and professional learning environment where students can develop practical communication skills and achieve their academic and professional goals.
